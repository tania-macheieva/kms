## CDS: зміна амінокислоти
- missense_variant: A sequence variant, that changes one or more bases, resulting in a different amino acid sequence but where the length is preserved.
- conservative_missense_variant: A sequence variant whereby at least one base of a codon is changed resulting in a codon that encodes for a different but similar amino acid. These variants may 
- non_conservative_missense_variant: A sequence variant whereby at least one base of a codon is changed resulting in a codon that encodes for an amino acid with different biochemical properties.
- nonsynonymous_variant: A non-synonymous variant is an inframe, protein altering variant, resulting in a codon change.
- protein_altering_variant: A sequence_variant which is predicted to change the protein encoded in the coding sequence.
- rare_amino_acid_variant: A sequence variant whereby at least one base of a codon encoding a rare amino acid is changed, resulting in a different encoded amino acid.
- pyrrolysine_loss: A sequence variant whereby at least one base of a codon encoding pyrrolysine is changed, resulting in a different encoded amino acid.
- selenocysteine_gain: A sequence variant whereby at least one base of a codon is changed, resulting in a selenocysteine.
- selenocysteine_loss: A sequence variant whereby at least one base of a codon encoding selenocysteine is changed, resulting in a different encoded amino acid.
- coding_sequence_variant: A sequence variant that changes the coding sequence.
## CDS: без зміни білка
- synonymous_variant: A sequence variant where there is no resulting change to the encoded amino acid.
- stop_retained_variant: A sequence variant where at least one base in the terminator codon is changed, but the terminator remains.
- start_retained_variant: A sequence variant where at least one base in the start codon is changed, but the start remains.
## CDS: зсув рамки
- frameshift_variant: A sequence variant which causes a disruption of the translational reading frame, because the number of nucleotides inserted or deleted is not a multiple of thre
- minus_1_frameshift_variant: A sequence variant which causes a disruption of the translational reading frame, by shifting one base ahead.
- minus_2_frameshift_variant: A sequence variant which causes a disruption of the translational reading frame, by shifting two bases forward.
- plus_1_frameshift_variant: A sequence variant which causes a disruption of the translational reading frame, by shifting one base backward.
- plus_2_frameshift_variant: A sequence variant which causes a disruption of the translational reading frame, by shifting two bases backward.
- frameshift_elongation: A frameshift variant that causes the translational reading frame to be extended relative to the reference feature.
- frameshift_truncation: A frameshift variant that causes the translational reading frame to be shortened relative to the reference feature.
- frame_restoring_variant: A sequence variant that reverts the sequence of a previous frameshift mutation back to the initial frame.
- frameshift_variant_NMD_escaping: A frameshift_variant (SO:0001589) that allows the transcript to escape nonsense-mediated decay (NMD).
- frameshift_variant_NMD_triggering: A frameshift_variant (SO:0001589) that is degraded by nonsense-mediated decay (NMD).
## CDS: інфрейм-індели
- inframe_variant: A sequence variant which does not cause a disruption of the translational reading frame.
- inframe_indel: A coding sequence variant where the change does not alter the frame of the transcript.
- inframe_insertion: An inframe non synonymous variant that inserts bases into in the coding sequence.
- inframe_deletion: An inframe non synonymous variant that deletes bases from the coding sequence.
- conservative_inframe_deletion: An inframe decrease in cds length that deletes one or more entire codons from the coding sequence but does not change any remaining codons.
- conservative_inframe_insertion: An inframe increase in cds length that inserts one or more codons into the coding sequence between existing codons.
- disruptive_inframe_deletion: An inframe decrease in cds length that deletes bases from the coding sequence starting within an existing codon.
- disruptive_inframe_insertion: An inframe increase in cds length that inserts one or more codons into the coding sequence within an existing codon.
## CDS: старт/стоп-кодон
- initiator_codon_variant: A codon variant that changes at least one base of the first codon of a transcript.
- start_lost: A codon variant that changes at least one base of the canonical start codon.
- stop_gained: A sequence variant whereby at least one base of a codon is changed, resulting in a premature stop codon, leading to a shortened polypeptide.
- stop_lost: A sequence variant where at least one base of the terminator codon (stop) is changed, resulting in an elongated transcript.
- terminator_codon_variant: A sequence variant whereby at least one of the bases in the terminator codon is changed.
- stop_gained_NMD_escaping: A stop_gained (SO:0001587) variant that allows the transcript to escape nonsense-mediated decay (NMD).
- stop_gained_NMD_triggering: A stop_gained (SO:0001587) variant that is degraded by nonsense-mediated decay (NMD).
- redundant_inserted_stop_gained: A sequence variant whereby at least one base of a codon is changed, resulting in a stop codon inserted next to an existing stop codon. This leads to a polypepti
- incomplete_terminal_codon_variant: A sequence variant where at least one base of the final codon of an incompletely annotated transcript is changed.
## Сплайсинг: канонічні сайти
- splice_acceptor_variant: A splice variant that changes the 2 base region at the 3' end of an intron.
- splice_donor_variant: A splice variant that changes the 2 base pair region at the 5' end of an intron.
- splice_site_variant: A sequence variant that changes the first two or last two bases of an intron, or the 5th base from the start of the intron in the orientation of the transcript.
- splice_acceptor_variant_NMD_escaping: A splice_acceptor_variant (SO:0001574) that allows the transcript to escape nonsense-mediated decay (NMD).
- splice_acceptor_variant_NMD_triggering: A splice_acceptor_variant (SO:0001574) that is degraded by nonsense-mediated decay (NMD).
- splice_donor_variant_NMD_escaping: A splice_donor_variant (SO:0001575) that allows the transcript to escape nonsense-mediated decay (NMD).
- splice_donor_variant_NMD_triggering: A splice_donor_variant (SO:0001575) that is degraded by nonsense-mediated decay (NMD).
## Сплайсинг: прилеглі регіони
- splice_region_variant: A sequence variant in which a change has occurred within the region of the splice site, either within 1-3 bases of the exon or 3-8 bases of the intron.
- splice_donor_5th_base_variant: A sequence variant that causes a change at the 5th base pair after the start of the intron in the orientation of the transcript.
- splice_donor_region_variant: A sequence variant that falls in the region between the 3rd and 6th base after splice junction (5' end of intron).
- splice_polypyrimidine_tract_variant: A sequence variant that falls in the polypyrimidine tract at 3' end of intron between 17 and 3 bases from the end (acceptor -3 to acceptor -17).
- exonic_splice_region_variant: A sequence variant in which a change has occurred within the exonic region of the splice site, 1-2 bases from boundary.
- extended_intronic_splice_region_variant: A sequence variant occurring in the intron, within 10 bases of exon.
- non_coding_transcript_splice_region_variant: A transcript variant occurring within the splice region (1-3 bases of the exon or 3-8 bases of the intron) of a non coding transcript.
## Сплайсинг: нові сайти й екзони
- cryptic_splice_acceptor: A sequence variant whereby a new splice site is created due to the activation of a new acceptor.
- cryptic_splice_donor: A sequence variant whereby a new splice site is created due to the activation of a new donor.
- cryptic_splice_site_variant: A sequence variant causing a new (functional) splice site.
- splicing_variant: A sequence variant that changes the process of splicing.
- exon_loss_variant: A sequence variant whereby an exon is lost from the transcript.
- intron_gain_variant: A sequence variant whereby an intron is gained by the processed transcript; usually a result of an alteration of the donor or acceptor.
- pseudoexon_variant: Inclusion of a non-conserved, unannotated exon in the spliced transcript, as a consequence of a variant in the gene.

## Некодувальні РНК
